How Flamingo Began

A clinic that started from a real patient need.

Flamingo Medical and Wellness began with a simple passion — helping people feel healthier, happier, and more confident in themselves.

After many years of working within the NHS as an Advanced Nurse Practitioner, our founder noticed that many patients were struggling to access safe, trusted and professional ear care services after ear irrigation gradually stopped being widely offered through the NHS.

This inspired the creation of Essex Body Cleanse, initially specialising in ear microsuction and professional ear care, before growing into Flamingo Medical and Wellness.
