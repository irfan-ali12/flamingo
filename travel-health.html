Malaria & Destination Risks

Protection beyond vaccines.

Some destinations may require malaria prevention advice. This can include bite avoidance, awareness of risk areas and suitable preventative medication where clinically appropriate.

Your risk depends on where you are travelling, the length of your stay, activities planned, accommodation type and personal health history.
