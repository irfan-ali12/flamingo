Frequently Asked Questions

What is global parts sourcing? +

It is the process of finding, qualifying, and managing international suppliers for parts, components, and assemblies.

Which countries do you source from? +

EB supports sourcing across China, Taiwan, Vietnam, India, Mexico, and other global manufacturing regions.

How do you qualify overseas suppliers? +

We review certifications, capabilities, production history, sample quality, facility standards, and supplier communication.

How do you handle quality control? +

We support inspections, production checks, compliance reviews, sample validation, and ongoing supplier oversight.

Can you manage turnkey manufacturing projects? +

Yes. We can support sourcing, prototyping, quoting, production management, quality assurance, logistics, and delivery.

Can you support custom assemblies? +

Yes. EB supports custom components, subassemblies, molded parts, machined parts, electronic components, and finished assemblies.
