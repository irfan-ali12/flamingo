CryoPen

Precise cryotherapy for unwanted skin lesions.

CryoPen is an advanced, precision device that uses a fine jet of nitrous oxide to freeze and destroy unwanted skin lesions quickly and safely.

Unlike traditional freezing methods, CryoPen delivers targeted cryotherapy, meaning only the lesion is treated with minimal impact on surrounding skin.
