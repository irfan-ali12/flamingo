CryoPen Precision Cryotherapy

CryoPen uses a fine jet of nitrous oxide to freeze and destroy unwanted benign skin lesions quickly and safely. It targets only the lesion, treats many concerns in seconds and minimises impact on surrounding skin.

Treats:
Skin tags, warts, verrucas, sun spots, cherry angiomas, milia, seborrhoeic keratosis and pigmented spots.
Benefits:
Quick treatment, precision targeting, minimal discomfort, no downtime and natural healing.

FAQ

Clear answers before you book.

Do I need travel vaccinations for my trip?

Not always, and that is exactly why a consultation matters. Your destination, length of stay, activities, medical history and previous vaccines all affect what you need.

How far in advance should I book a travel consultation?

Ideally 6–8 weeks before travel. This allows time for vaccines to become fully effective and for multi-dose courses if required. Last-minute appointments may still help.

What happens during a travel consultation?

We review your destination, itinerary, duration, type of travel, medical history and previous vaccinations, then provide clear guidance aligned with Department of Health recommendations.
